Alomos

Alomos is a Singapore-based e-commerce newcomer aiming to become the trusted one-stop shop for electronics and gadgets — a bold goal in a market already dominated by well-known players. As a new entrant with no brand recognition yet, the challenge was clear: how do you convince customers to trust an unfamiliar name over competitors they already know?

I helped Alomos build that trust from the ground up, shaping their brand experience, brand guidelines, website, and design system. Differentiating their offering in a crowded marketplace meant defining a clear unique selling proposition and pairing it with high-quality visuals that could carry a premium brand identity — turning “unknown newcomer” into a credible, trustworthy option for customers.
